Settings

Set up the camera

  • Placement: 2–3 m away at hip height, about 45° to your side. Your head, hands and feet should all be visible, at the bar and on the floor.
  • Pull-ups count when your mouth rises above your hands, after a full hang with straight arms.
  • Push-ups count when you lock out again after your elbows bend to about 100°. Side-on to the camera works best.
  • Air squats count when you stand up tall after your hips drop to knee height.
  • The app moves on to the next movement by itself once you finish the reps for the current one.
  • Video never leaves your device. Pose detection runs in this browser tab.
